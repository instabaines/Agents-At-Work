DEMO SCENARIO: HIGHER EDUCATION
This scenario is optimized for university and college audiences focused on
student services, administrative efficiency, and limited staffing capacity.

OUR PRODUCT
OrbitFlow is presented here as an AI agent platform for higher-education
administration, student operations, and cross-department process automation.

Core buyer:
- Vice President of Operations
- CIO
- Enrollment operations leader
- Student services leader
- Shared services or HR operations director

Why this scenario matters:
- Institutions run many repetitive coordination workflows with small teams
- Student experience depends on timely routing and follow-up
- Budgets are tight, so ease of deployment and staffing efficiency matter

KEY WORKFLOWS
- Admissions application triage and routing
- Student support case intake and escalation
- Financial aid document follow-up
- Faculty and staff onboarding coordination
- Procurement and approval workflow automation

BUYING CRITERIA
- Ease of use for administrative teams
- Integration with email, ticketing, identity, and SIS-adjacent systems
- Clear ROI without enterprise-consulting overhead
- Governance that is strong enough without slowing adoption

COMPETITOR LANDSCAPE
FLOWMESH EDU
- Type: no-code automation with AI assistants
- Pricing: platform tiers plus workflow volume
- Strength: approachable for operations staff
- Weakness: limited adaptive reasoning for messy, exception-heavy cases

STACKPILOT
- Type: developer-first agent platform
- Pricing: monthly platform fee plus usage
- Strength: flexible for technical innovation teams
- Weakness: too technical for most administrative departments

SERVICE CAMPUS AI
- Type: student service copilot
- Pricing: per-seat or per-case packaging
- Strength: fast value for one service function
- Weakness: not broad enough for institution-wide operations

ENTERPRISEPATH
- Type: enterprise transformation platform
- Pricing: high annual commitments
- Strength: governance and executive polish
- Weakness: hard to justify when budgets and headcount are constrained

WHERE ORBITFLOW WINS
- Easier to operate than developer-centric tools
- Broader than function-specific student-service products
- More adaptive than basic automation builders

POSITIONING MESSAGE
"AI workflow automation for institutions that need more service capacity without adding staff."
