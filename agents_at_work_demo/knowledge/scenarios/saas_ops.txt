DEMO SCENARIO: SAAS OPERATIONS
This scenario is optimized for audiences discussing mid-market SaaS growth,
shared services, and internal business process automation.

OUR PRODUCT
OrbitFlow is an AI agent platform for mid-market SaaS companies that want to
automate internal business workflows without building a heavy internal AI stack.

Core buyer:
- COO
- VP Operations
- Head of RevOps
- Support operations leader
- IT operations lead

Why this scenario matters:
- SaaS operators want efficiency gains without a long implementation cycle
- Cross-functional handoffs create measurable friction
- Teams often use many systems but have lean internal operations staff

KEY WORKFLOWS
- Lead qualification and routing across CRM, enrichment, and SDR queues
- Support triage, summarization, and escalation
- Customer onboarding workflow coordination
- Employee onboarding and access provisioning
- Approval and exception routing between revenue and finance teams

MARKET DYNAMICS
- Buyers want ROI inside one quarter
- They prefer packaged outcomes over flexible but abstract platforms
- AI is attractive, but trust and governance still matter

COMPETITOR LANDSCAPE
STACKPILOT
- Type: developer-first agent orchestration
- Pricing: starts near $2,500/month plus usage
- Strength: flexibility, APIs, advanced control
- Weakness: too technical for most business operators

PIPELANE AI
- Type: GTM workflow copilot
- Pricing: roughly $89-$149 per user/month
- Strength: strong sales and RevOps templates
- Weakness: weak outside revenue workflows

FLOWMESH
- Type: automation suite with AI add-ons
- Pricing: tiered, often $1,200-$5,000+/month
- Strength: broad connectors and familiar automation model
- Weakness: AI can feel bolted on and pricing gets confusing

ENTERPRISEPATH
- Type: services-heavy enterprise platform
- Pricing: annual commitments often start around $120,000
- Strength: governance and enterprise credibility
- Weakness: too slow and expensive for many mid-market buyers

WHERE ORBITFLOW WINS
- Easier than developer-first platforms
- Broader than department-specific copilots
- More AI-native than legacy automation products
- Faster to launch than services-heavy enterprise offerings

POSITIONING MESSAGE
"AI agents for internal operations without a six-month implementation."
