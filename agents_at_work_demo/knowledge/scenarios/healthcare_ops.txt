DEMO SCENARIO: HEALTHCARE OPERATIONS
This scenario is optimized for audiences interested in patient operations,
care coordination, back-office workflows, and higher governance expectations.

OUR PRODUCT
OrbitFlow is positioned here as an AI agent platform for healthcare operations
teams that need workflow automation with strong human approval points.

Core buyer:
- COO
- VP Operations
- Director of Care Operations
- Revenue cycle leader
- IT and compliance stakeholders

Why this scenario matters:
- Healthcare organizations have expensive coordination overhead
- Many processes involve routing, summarization, and exception handling
- Leaders need efficiency gains without sacrificing oversight

KEY WORKFLOWS
- Referral intake and routing
- Prior authorization follow-up coordination
- Patient scheduling exception handling
- Revenue cycle case triage and document chasing
- Internal employee onboarding for clinical and administrative staff

BUYING CRITERIA
- Auditability and approval controls
- Integration with EHR-adjacent and ticketing workflows
- Role-based access and traceability
- Clear boundaries for human review on sensitive actions

COMPETITOR LANDSCAPE
COMPLIANCECOPILOT
- Type: governance-first workflow AI
- Pricing: often $4,000-$8,000/month
- Strength: auditability and controls
- Weakness: rigid experience and slower business adoption

ENTERPRISEPATH
- Type: enterprise-grade AI transformation platform
- Pricing: large annual commitments with services
- Strength: executive confidence and white-glove delivery
- Weakness: long timeline before value is visible

FLOWMESH HEALTH
- Type: legacy automation with AI add-ons
- Pricing: platform tiers plus AI usage
- Strength: familiar no-code workflow builder
- Weakness: weak handling of ambiguous, exception-heavy operational flows

HELPDESK IQ
- Type: support-focused operational AI
- Pricing: per-seat plus automation volume
- Strength: efficient service operations experiences
- Weakness: narrow scope outside service desk workflows

WHERE ORBITFLOW WINS
- Stronger business usability than governance-first tools
- Faster launch than enterprise transformation programs
- Better support for cross-functional operations than narrow point solutions

POSITIONING MESSAGE
"Operational AI for healthcare teams that need speed with control."
