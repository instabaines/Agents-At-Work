DEMO SCENARIO: NONPROFIT OPERATIONS
This scenario is optimized for nonprofit and mission-driven audiences that care
about staff efficiency, donor stewardship, and program delivery capacity.

OUR PRODUCT
OrbitFlow is framed here as an AI agent platform that helps nonprofit teams
automate internal operations while protecting limited staff time.

Core buyer:
- COO
- Chief of Staff
- Operations director
- Development operations lead
- Program operations manager

Why this scenario matters:
- Teams are lean and often run many critical workflows manually
- Administrative overhead pulls time away from mission delivery
- Budget sensitivity increases pressure for clear ROI and simple deployment

KEY WORKFLOWS
- Donor and grant follow-up coordination
- Volunteer onboarding and communications routing
- Program intake review and case assignment
- Internal HR, procurement, and approval workflows
- Board reporting preparation and operational summary generation

BUYING CRITERIA
- Clear time savings for small teams
- Ease of rollout without full-time technical staff
- Pricing predictability
- Enough governance for confidence, without enterprise complexity

COMPETITOR LANDSCAPE
PIPELANE AI
- Type: narrow revenue-team workflow copilot
- Pricing: per-seat
- Strength: clear value in fundraising-adjacent outreach workflows
- Weakness: weak fit for broader nonprofit operations

FLOWMESH
- Type: automation suite with AI layers
- Pricing: tiered plus usage
- Strength: lots of integrations and automation familiarity
- Weakness: AI still feels secondary for complex operational judgment

STACKPILOT
- Type: flexible developer-first orchestration
- Pricing: platform fee plus usage
- Strength: high flexibility
- Weakness: unrealistic for many lean nonprofit teams to own internally

ENTERPRISEPATH
- Type: enterprise transformation platform
- Pricing: large annual commitment
- Strength: mature governance story
- Weakness: too expensive and too heavy for most nonprofit realities

WHERE ORBITFLOW WINS
- Stronger cross-functional value than departmental tools
- Faster time to value than heavy enterprise platforms
- Easier ownership model than developer-first systems

POSITIONING MESSAGE
"Operational leverage for mission-driven teams without adding overhead."
