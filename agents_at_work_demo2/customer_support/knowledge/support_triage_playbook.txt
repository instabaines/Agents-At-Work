DEMO 2: CUSTOMER SUPPORT TRIAGE PLAYBOOK

PURPOSE
This playbook helps a support triage swarm classify incoming customer messages,
assign urgency, route them to the correct team, and flag risky or ambiguous cases.

ISSUE CATEGORIES
Billing
- Invoices, charges, payment failures, refunds, credits, taxes, plan confusion

Bug
- Product behavior not working as intended, errors, outages, broken workflows, failed integrations

Account
- Login problems, SSO issues, password resets, access requests, permissions, account lockouts

Other
- General questions, feature requests, vague requests, or non-support messages

PRIORITY GUIDANCE
Low
- Minor inconvenience
- Workaround exists
- Single-user question with low business impact

Medium
- A team is slowed down but not fully blocked
- Limited business impact
- Important but not urgent request

High
- A critical workflow is blocked
- Deadline pressure exists
- Multiple users are impacted
- Billing access, login access, or outage concerns are present

ROUTING RULES
Billing -> Billing Operations
Bug -> Product Support Engineering
Account -> Identity and Access Support
Other -> General Support Queue

OVERRIDES
- If the message mentions SSO, permissions, lockout, or login, route to Identity and Access Support
- If the message mentions invoices, charges, payment, refund, or billing history, route to Billing Operations
- If the message mentions outage, bug, broken integration, or failed workflow, route to Product Support Engineering
- If the issue is unclear, route to General Support Queue and flag for supervisor review

SUPERVISOR REVIEW TRIGGERS
- The message could fit multiple categories
- Priority is unclear
- Executive visibility or deadline pressure is mentioned
- The suggested routing may be risky or incomplete

STYLE
- Be concise
- Make the reason for category and urgency explicit
- Prefer safe escalation when the case is ambiguous
- Optimize for fast routing, not long analysis

EXAMPLES
Example 1
Message: "We were billed twice this month and cannot download the corrected invoice."
- Category: Billing
- Priority: Medium
- Route: Billing Operations

Example 2
Message: "Our team cannot log in after enabling Okta enforcement."
- Category: Account
- Priority: High
- Route: Identity and Access Support

Example 3
Message: "The Slack integration is failing and our support workflow stopped."
- Category: Bug
- Priority: High
- Route: Product Support Engineering
